Synth-Flow Pro

All values are simulated locally from hypothetical parameters. Not safety-certified; not for real vehicle control. E-STOP only halts this software.

Every controller gets the identical scenario, start state and parameters from the Command Center sliders (no faults injected). Results are computed, never preset; the highlighted row per scenario has the lowest unmet energy (ties included) and is not a ranking claim.

Model (SI units internally)

  • Hydrogen-derived power: P_avail = P_rated(500 W) · availability · w. Warm-up w follows w' = (1−w)/τ (τ 25 s). A mathematical abstraction of LOHC delivery, not dehydrogenation chemistry.
  • Fuel cell: output ramps toward the command (up-rate slider, down 80 W/s), never above P_avail, and drops to 0 below the minimum stable output. Illustrative η = 0.60 − 0.20·P/P_rated; loss = P/η − P.
  • Smart Skin battery (conceptual): 20 Wh; V_oc = 10.5 + 1.5·SOC; I = P/V_oc; loss = I²R (R 0.02 Ω); ΔSOC = −(P+loss)Δt/E; C·dT/dt = loss + hA(T_amb − T) (C 100 J/K, hA 3 W/K). Limits: 400 W discharge, 200 W charge, SOC window 10–95% with a 5% taper, linear thermal derating from the slider temperature to 60 °C.
  • Bus balance each 1 s step: P_fc + P_batt = P_demand − P_unmet + P_surplus. Negative demand is regenerative braking; power the battery cannot accept is reported as surplus (friction brake or resistor), never silently dropped.
  • Plant limits are applied after the controller, so no controller can bypass battery or fuel-cell limits.

Controllers (all rule-based heuristics, no trained AI)

  • Fixed threshold: fuel cell on below 50% SOC, off above 70%.
  • Fuel-cell priority: FC follows demand.
  • Battery priority: FC runs only below the reserve SOC.
  • Demand-preview heuristic: FC pre-ramps to the larger of current demand and demand one horizon ahead.
  • Synth-Flow (research hypothesis, untested claim): estimates near-term shortfall = previewed demand − previewed H2-derived power, compares it with the battery's currently permitted discharge (which includes thermal derating) and SOC reserve, then pre-charges, shares or follows. Prediction can be switched off. It is not hardcoded to win; read the Controller Lab table as it comes out.

Glossary

LOHC
Liquid organic hydrogen carrier: a liquid that stores hydrogen and releases it when heated.
PEM fuel cell
Makes electricity from hydrogen across a proton-exchange membrane.
SOC
State of charge: battery energy left as a percentage.
Regenerative braking
Motor acts as a generator while slowing and sends power back to the battery.
Thermal derating
Reducing allowed power when a part gets too hot.
Hysteresis
Different on and off thresholds so a controller does not flicker.
Power vs energy
Power is a rate in W; energy is power over time (Wh).
